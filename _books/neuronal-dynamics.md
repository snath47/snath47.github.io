A comprehensive introduction to the dynamics of neurons and neural networks, from single neuron models to large-scale network dynamics. Covers the Hodgkin-Huxley formalism, integrate-and-fire models, spike-timing dependent plasticity, and population dynamics.

This book is freely available online from EPFL and is a cornerstone text for anyone working in computational neuroscience.
