The classic introduction to nonlinear dynamics and chaos, with applications across physics, biology, chemistry, and engineering. Strogatz's intuitive approach makes bifurcations, limit cycles, chaos, and strange attractors accessible through geometric reasoning and compelling examples. A must-read for anyone working with dynamical systems.
