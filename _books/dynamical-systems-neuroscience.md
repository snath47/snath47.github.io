A rigorous exploration of the geometry of excitability and bursting in neurons using dynamical systems theory. Izhikevich introduces his celebrated simple neuron model and provides geometric insights into spiking and bursting dynamics through bifurcation analysis. Essential reading for understanding the mathematics behind neural computation.
