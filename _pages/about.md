I'm a Master's candidate in **Data Science** at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), with a Bachelor's degree from Canada. I have research experience in the fields of **efficient and brain inpired machine learning**, **deep learning**, **nonlinear dynamics of spiking neural networks**, **reservoir computing** and **reinforcement learning**.

My ongoing Master's thesis, supervised by Prof. Dr. Yamakou, investigates how structural heterogeneity shapes noise-driven signal enhancement in networks of coupled FitzHugh-Nagumo neurons. I have also designed Echo State Networks to forecast chaotic bursting in Hindmarsh-Rose neurons and studied physical nonlinear oscillators as computational substrates through Liquid State Machines. My background further includes hands-on research experience in reinforcement learning and deep learning

On the technical side, I'm proficient in **Python**, **MATLAB**, and **C++/Java**, and experienced with **JAX**, **PyTorch**, and **Scikit-Learn**. I manage large-scale numerical simulations on **HPC clusters** using SLURM as a part of my Master Thesis. My work spans from Fraunhofer IIS, where I build world models with LSTMs, to the University of Alberta, where I implemented YOLO-based object detection for Arctic wildlife monitoring during my bachelor degree.

Beyond research, I bring cross-disciplinary experience from Siemens Energy, where as Data Science developer, I worked with Electrical Engineers to automate their processes. I aim to work at the forefront of efficient and brain-inspired AI building systems inspired by neural processes. If you share this vision or have a collaboration in mind, I'd love to hear from you at [sandipan.nath@fau.de](mailto:sandipan.nath@fau.de).
